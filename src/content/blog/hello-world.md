This site is a small home for projects, writing, and experiments by Haowei Chan.

The goal is simple: keep the portfolio lightweight, keep the blog easy to publish, and leave a public trail of what is being built and learned along the way.

Astro handles the site shell, while Markdown keeps writing close to the codebase.

Most posts here will be project notes, because good project notes make future work easier: compact writeups about design choices, implementation details, and the small decisions that shape a product over time. Expect product experiments, web tooling, deployment notes, and lessons from maintaining public projects.
